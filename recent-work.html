The Work

Over three weeks, I documented Hank Design Studios as they prepared and built their installation for RenderATL and Atlanta Tech Week, capturing the process from behind the scenes through the final conference experience.

I followed the team throughout the build and event, capturing both photo and video of their work, interactions, and the finished experience. In post-production, I edited long- and short-form video content and delivered a final gallery of 100 edited photos—creating a full visual story of the project from prep to execution.
